In short: StatMe has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number. It's free for fans of all ages because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Users under 18 only get non-personalized ads and are never asked to allow tracking. Adults get personalized ads only if they allow tracking, and can change their choice at any time.

This policy explains what information the StatMe app ("StatMe", "we", "us") and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

1. Information we don't collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • No payment information. StatMe is free.
  • Your exact date of birth. StatMe asks only for your year of birth, and it stays on your device (see section 2).

4. Your choices

  • Tracking (adults): iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (or Settings → StatMe) lets you allow or deny tracking at any time.
  • Privacy choices: tap the settings (gear) icon in the top-right of StatMe's Events screen and choose Privacy choices to review or change your ad consent. Where Google's consent tools apply, this includes the EEA/UK consent form and the US state opt-out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising.
  • Notifications and promotional reminders: turn them off in StatMe or in iPhone Settings → Notifications → StatMe. Promotional reminders are only sent if you opted in, and are never offered to users under 13.
  • Stop all collection: deleting the app removes the information stored on your device and stops any further collection by StatMe and AdMob through the app.

5. Sale, sharing and targeted advertising (US state privacy laws)

We don't sell your personal information for money. For adults who allow it, showing personalized ads through AdMob may count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or as "targeted advertising" or a "sale", under some US state laws, including those of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon. You can opt out at any time using Privacy choices or by denying tracking. We don't knowingly sell or share the personal information of users under 18, and never show them personalized ads.

6. Retention

Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, normally no more than 30 days. Notification tokens are deleted when you turn notifications off or when Apple reports the token is no longer valid. Information stored on your device stays there until you change it or delete the app. Google keeps advertising data under its own policies.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or US state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we process, and access a copy of it;
  • correct or delete it;
  • opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising (section 5);
  • withdraw consent;
  • not be treated differently for using these rights. StatMe works the same either way.

To make a request, email statmesupport@gmail.com. You may also use an authorized agent. StatMe has no accounts, so we usually can't link server data to a specific person. We'll explain what we can do and respond within the time the law requires, normally 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision. If we deny the appeal, you may contact your state attorney general. For data held by Google, use the tools linked in section 3.

8. Children

StatMe is a statistics app for fans of all ages. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for users under 13:

  • We don't ask children for their name, email or other contact details.
  • Children only receive non-personalized, child-directed ads, are never asked to allow tracking, and are never offered promotional reminders.
  • If a child turns on notifications, the push token is used only to deliver the fight alerts they chose.
  • Technical data such as IP addresses and device details is used only to run the app, keep it secure and serve child-directed ads, as COPPA permits.

Parents or guardians can contact us at statmesupport@gmail.com to ask about, or request deletion of, any information related to their child. Deleting the app also removes the information stored on the device. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13 in a way COPPA doesn't allow, we delete it.

9. Security and international transfers

Data between the app and our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with appropriate safeguards where the law requires them.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: email statmesupport@gmail.com.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date above. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
