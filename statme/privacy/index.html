Privacy Policy

StatMe – Live Fight Stats (iOS) · Effective 5 November 2026

In short: StatMe has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number. The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising data to show and measure ads. Ads are personalised and tracked only if you allow it, and you can change your choice at any time.

This policy explains what information the StatMe app ("StatMe", "we", "us") and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have. StatMe is operated by the developer named on its App Store listing.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • No payment information. StatMe has no in-app purchases at this time.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

StatMe uses Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC, to show ads. Google may collect and use the data described above under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Apple App Tracking Transparency. StatMe asks whether you allow tracking. If you don't allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) isn't shared and ads aren't personalised using tracking across other companies' apps and websites. You may still see ads that aren't personalised.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Where the law requires, a consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads before any personalised ads are requested.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (or Settings → StatMe) lets you allow or deny tracking at any time.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): open the menu in the top-right of StatMe's Events screen and choose Privacy choices to review or change your consent.
  • Notifications: turn them off in StatMe or in iPhone Settings → Notifications → StatMe.
  • Stop all collection: deleting the app stops any further collection by StatMe and AdMob through the app.

5. Retention

Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, normally no more than 30 days. Notification tokens are deleted when you turn notifications off or when Apple reports the token is no longer valid. Google keeps advertising data under its own policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. StatMe has no accounts, so we usually can't link server data to a specific person. Contact us (section 9) and we'll help where we can. For data held by Google, use the tools linked in section 3.

7. Children

StatMe isn't directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has used StatMe and shared personal information, contact us.

8. Security and international transfers

Data between the app and our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with appropriate safeguards where the law requires them.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: email statmesupport@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
